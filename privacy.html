Privacy

Access-request data is only used to provision Linje.

When you request access by email, Linje uses the information you send to respond and provision access to the portal and project setup flow. The public site may also collect basic, non-sensitive page analytics when analytics are configured.

To request removal of access-request data, email hello@linje.systems.
