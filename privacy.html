Privacy

Account and analytics data stay narrowly scoped.

Linje uses your account email to deliver magic links and operate your portal account. It is not included in PostHog events.

When analytics are configured, the public site records page views, the market surface that led to the visit, campaign parameters, and explicit call-to-action clicks. Session replay and automatic interaction capture are disabled. Query strings are removed from captured page and referrer URLs, IP addresses are discarded, and Linje does not send email addresses, message contents, attachment names, or application metadata to public-site analytics.

PostHog runs in cookieless mode. The first market surface and campaign are kept in the browser-tab session and passed to signup as bounded first-touch attribution. Product events use opaque identifiers and never include customer email content or application metadata. Google Search Console provides aggregate search-query and page-performance data for the site.

To request removal of account or analytics data, email hello@linje.systems.
