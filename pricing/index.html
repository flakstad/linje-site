Counting

Messages are counted in both directions.

Outbound

Each recipient delivery submitted through Linje counts as one message.

Inbound

Each inbound email Linje accepts and stores counts as one message.

Webhooks and replay

Delivery attempts, retries, and replay do not count as additional messages.

Reply routes

Creating and keeping reply addresses does not consume message usage. A customer's reply counts once when Linje accepts it as an inbound message.
